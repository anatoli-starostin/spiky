A clean 3-layer spiking net that computes a LUT — smart-initialized, then evolved

Contents · LUT smart-initialization (no scaffolding, no STDP)

The earlier demos build the exact LUT→spiking circuit with heavy scaffolding — a clock, complement neurons, a one-hot row decoder. This one throws ALL of that away. It's a minimal three-layer feed-forward net — latency-in → detectors → latency-out — seeded directly from the trained LUT's hyperplanes (tau-b ≈ 0.53 at initialization), then refined by plain weight + delay evolution (NO STDP, no learning-at-inference). The 6 real-valued inputs are latency-coded (bigger value fires earlier); the 4 outputs' first-spike order is meant to reproduce the LUT's stored-value ordering. It obeys Dale's law (a neuron is excitatory or inhibitory).

The evolved network

Inputs (cyan squares, left) → hidden detectors (green excitatory / red inhibitory, middle) → 4 outputs (ringed, right). Edge thickness ∝ weight; each edge carries an evolved conduction delay. No clock, no complement neurons, no row decoder — just the clean 3 layers.

Watch it compute — interactive

Pick an input, then watch the net's real recorded spikes play out: the 6 inputs fire at their latencies, signals travel along the synapses (respecting each edge's delay), the detectors fire, and the 4 outputs fire in an order. The inputs are curated best→worst by tau-b so you can see it get some exactly right and others only roughly ordered — an honest picture of a ~0.78-tau-b mapping.

Moving dots = spikes propagating along synapses (green = excitatory, red = inhibitory); a node's halo = it just spiked. Every spike time is the actual output of the evolved net — nothing is fabricated.

Why this matters

This closes the loop from the hand-built exact construction to the evolved substrate: a trained lookup table can be compiled into a tiny spiking net by seeding from its hyperplanes and evolving away the leak/threshold distortion — no scaffolding, no plasticity, just weights and delays. The result is a precise-ish, compact feed-forward spiking mapping (tau-b ~0.78, exact-order-match ~55% on real-valued inputs), and it stays small — it pruned below even the seed. Smart initialization turns evolution from a from-scratch search into a quick recovery of a known solution.
