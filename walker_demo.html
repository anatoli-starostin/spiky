Walker2d LUT policy → spiking network

Contents · Real spnet spike trace, in the inspector

The trained Walker2d SAC control policy (4-bit LUT actor) compiled into a spiking network and loaded into the same graph/activity inspector as the other demos. Pick an observation (the input selector) to load its REAL recorded spnet spike trace; the graph animates the actual spikes propagating and the raster shows every neuron's firing over the 256-tick window (drag to scrub). Layers: 17 obs inputs → per-table hyperplane comparators → 1-of-64 address neurons → 6 action outputs. A representative 8 of the 32 tables are shown; comparators that never cross threshold stay dark (bit 0). The panel compares each action's LUT-oracle value to the value decoded from the output-neuron spike time.

Click a node or edge

Nodes are coloured by layer; edges by weight sign (blue −, red +), thickness by |weight|, dashed = larger delay. Click a comparator/address/output for its fire ticks and fields.
