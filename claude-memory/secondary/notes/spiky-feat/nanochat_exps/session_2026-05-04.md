## Experiments run this session

### Phase A — nap-vs-tph ablation on exp134 (468M nap=8 saturated baseline)

Tested whether each module's nap=8 was needed at saturated pair coverage.
Forks of exp134 keeping pair coverage ~100%, swapping nap=8→6 with tph compensation.

| Exp | Module changed nap=8→6 | Δ params | Final Δ bpb | Verdict |
|-----|------------------------|----------|--------------|---------|
| exp139 | qk only | −113M | (stopped @ 2400, +0.019 behind) | hurts noticeably |
| exp140 | v only | −38M | (stopped @ 2400, +0.023 behind) | hurts noticeably |
| **exp141** | out_proj only | **−151M** | **+0.004** at end | nearly free, kept |

**Finding**: out_proj tolerates nap reduction at saturated coverage; qk and v don't. Out_proj input is H·d_v=192, the largest module, so reducing nap there saves the most.

### Phase B — single-knob improvements on exp141

| Exp | Change | Final bpb | Δ vs exp141 |
|-----|--------|-----------|-------------|
| exp142 | MLP unembedder (LN→Lin(384, 1536)→ReLU→Lin(1536, vocab)) | 1.7278 | +0.003 worse despite +38M |
| **exp143** | attn_scale_init 0.25→0.4 | **1.7213** | **−0.004 better** |

**Finding**: 0.25 was under-init for the d_qk=32, ±1 dominance attention regime. Per-layer learned values from exp141 had been L0=0.43, L1=0.47, L2=0.40, L3=0.34, L4=0.28, L5=0.28 — all above the theoretical 1/√d_qk=0.177 (because the 496-dim dominance vectors are correlated, not i.i.d., so effective dim is smaller than 496).

**Finding (negative)**: MLP unembedder helped early (−0.016 at step 2800) but plateaued sooner. Crossover at step 7000-7200, ended +0.003 worse. Single linear unembedder is sufficient at this scale.

### Phase C — Channel-1 capacity probes on exp143 (the new 317M baseline)

Hypothesis: Channel-1 (table cell content) capacity is the bottleneck. Test by widening tph in each module.

| Exp | Change | Final bpb | Δ vs exp143 |
|-----|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **exp144** | qk_tph 256→384 (+75M) | **1.7101** | **−0.011** (huge) |
| exp145 | v_tph 256→384 (+38M, on top of exp144) | (stopped @ 3600, only −0.001 ahead of exp144) | saturated |

**Finding**: qk was significantly under-parameterized. v was already saturated. The asymmetry makes architectural sense:
- qk feeds attention scoring; every bit of pair-sign precision compounds via softmax dynamic range
- v feeds attention content, then gets averaged across positions and re-mixed by out_proj — averaging absorbs v's per-table redundancy

### Phase D — alternative attention paths (all failed)

Hypothesis: maybe SDPA on raw real-valued Q/K (skipping V2D) is better. Required several knob attempts.

| Exp | Change | Best Δ vs exp144 | Verdict |
|-----|--------|-------------------|---------|
| exp146 (init=0.4) | D2V (Borda+LN) before SDPA, q/k 32-dim | +0.13 at step 400 | failed |
| exp146 (init=0.18) | same with 1/√d_qk init | +0.15 by step 800 | even worse |
| exp147 | real Q/K to SDPA, d_qk=32→64, no LN | +0.21 at step 400 | failed badly |
| exp147 (with LN) | + LayerNorm on Q/K | +0.14 by step 1400 | gap stable but bad |
| exp148 | qk_lut init_std 0.001→0.05 | +0.15 by step 2400 | bigger init didn't help |

**Theoretical finding**: dominance representation is **structurally load-bearing** for LUT-based attention, not a hack:

1. **Manifold structure**: V2D maps Q to a structured manifold (4×10⁹ valid permutation patterns out of 2^496 possible binaries). Optimization stays on this manifold; gradients align with content semantics.

2. **Magnitude-invariance**: scaling Q by α doesn't change V2D(Q). The optimizer doesn't waste capacity matching scales — only rank order matters.

3. **Bounded dynamic range**: q_dom · k_dom ∈ [−P, P]. Logits stay well-conditioned automatically.

4. **Redundant gradient flow**: a single qk_lut output q_i affects (d_qk−1)=31 pair signs, distributed across many k positions. Adam-friendly.

LUT outputs at init are noisy AND low-rank (only top-8 anchor dims matter per table). Real-Q/K SDPA can't bootstrap from this; V2D's structure compensates. Conclusion: the "rank-only attention" is a real architectural feature, not a bug.
