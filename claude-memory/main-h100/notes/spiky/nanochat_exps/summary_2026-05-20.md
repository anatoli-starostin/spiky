# LUT-LM Experiments — 2026-05-20

**Theme:** strategic shift to *better training of the hard-forward LUT model*. The soft-mixture forward (exp444, 1.4821) is an **upper-bound reference only** — it reintroduces ~2^NAP lookups + softmax per table and defeats the matmul-free goal, so it is not deployable. **exp428 (1.4983 bpb, bs=16, 8K steps, 89.4 M, NAP=6 everywhere) is the standing reference** for the real goal: single-lookup, multiplication-free inference.

All runs below fork exp428 and change exactly one thing; everything else (arch, bs=16, 8000 steps, cosine+10% warmup) is identical, so per-step comparison is fair.

## 1. exp445 — soft weight-grad is exactly neutral (the exp444 win is the FORWARD)

Took exp428's hard argmax forward but attributed the **weight** gradient across all 2^NAP rows via `sel_soft` (`enable_soft_weight_grad`) instead of `index_add` at the chosen row. x-gradient unchanged.

- Result **1.498315 vs exp428 1.498293 — Δ +0.00002 (pure noise)**. Tracked exp428 within noise at every eval; never tracked exp444.
- **Conclusion:** the dense (soft) weight gradient contributes *nothing*. The entire exp444 −16 mb win is the soft **forward** itself (a better function), not the gradient distribution. Per-row sparsity is therefore **not** the bs=16 bottleneck. Confirms the tiny-scale exp360 finding at 89 M.

## 2. exp446 — the real (effective) Adam lr, per layer

Instrumented exp428 to log the true per-step step `nominal_lr · m̂/(√v̂+ε)` and a within-entry / between-row / between-table variance decomposition, per (layer, module).

- **LUT modules realize only ~22 % of nominal** `lut_lr=1e-3` → real peak lr ≈ 2.2e-4 (the 1e-3 is mostly illusory). `tok_emb` most damped (~0.15); unembedder 0.55→0.21.
- **lr variance is within-entry (~95 %, across output channels), NOT between-row.** The "hot/cold row" starvation signature is small (3–8 %) and *shrinks* over training; transient and depth-ordered early (deep layers worst), uniform across layers by convergence.
- Independently corroborates exp445: row coverage is fine at bs=16; sparsity isn't the lever.

## 3. exp447–exp453 — LUT-param optimizer sweep

Swapped only the LUT param group's optimizer (non-LUT stay AdamW). Added `Lion` and `SignSGD` classes inline; `lut_optimizer` / `lut_betas` / `lut_momentum` config keys select them. lr for the sign family set to 2e-4 (matched to exp446's realized Adam lr); SGD lr set from the measured LUT grad RMS (2.1e-3).

**The spectrum (magnitude handling):** SGD step ∝ ‖grad‖ → signSGD/LION uniform `±lr` (sign only) → Adam per-coord `lr/√v̂`. signSGD/LION store 1 momentum tensor (½ Adam's m+v).

Key results:
- **signSGD/Signum (β=0.9) ≈ AdamW** (1.5023, +0.004) at **half the optimizer memory** — Adam's √v̂ magnitude adaptivity turns out *not* to be the lever on LUTs.
- **LION's default β₂=0.99 was its whole deficit**, not the sign step: LION(β₁=β₂=0.9) is *mathematically identical* to Signum (exp452 reproduced exp448 at every eval). On sparse LUT grads the slow β₂=0.99 stored momentum signs stale directions.
- **β₂ has a sweet spot, and 0.95 beats AdamW:** β₂=0.99 bad (~+0.05), β₂=0.9 ≡ Signum (≈Adam), **β₂=0.95 → 1.4967, −0.0016 below AdamW** (below Adam at *every* eval from step 600→8000). A moderate-memory stored momentum accumulates each row's sparse hits while β₁=0.9 keeps the step direction fresh — beating both Signum (no memory) and Adam (√v̂ damping).
- SGD+mom (lr 0.1) finished +0.034 with a striking decay-phase catch-up (+0.068 peak → +0.034 final); lr is critical and must come from grad scale (lr 0.01 stalled +0.21).

**exp453 (LION β=(0.9,0.95)) is the new bs=16 LUT-LM best — better than AdamW *and* half the optimizer-state memory.**

Caveats: the −0.0016 final margin is within single-eval noise (~±0.003), but the consistent sub-AdamW trajectory across ~35 evals makes it credible. One seed, 8K/bs=16 only.
