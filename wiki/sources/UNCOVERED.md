# Uncovered sources worth a decision

Sources that no wiki page mentions yet, that are not dropped or routed in [`SOURCES.md`](../SOURCES.md), and whose evidence is clear: the run folders or summaries say what was compared, and the comparison is a single change (or the source states the baseline itself). All numbers are single seeds; "old evaluation" is the one whose validation window depended on the batch size. Walker2d and RL are deferred and not listed. The maintainers decide which to cover.

## [`architectures/transformers/lut-attention.md`](../architectures/transformers/lut-attention.md)

1. **One read-out table group against per-layer residual tables.** [`exp588`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp588_read_out_lut/config.json#L3) removes the residual table group of each of the 6 layers and reads the final stream once with one group of 6 times the tables; fork of [`exp587`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp587_E384_restph256) with the same 279.97M parameters: 1.4344 against 1.4471 (old evaluation, 8k steps).
   Belongs to "Shape of the tables": it is the only result on where the residual tables sit, at identical parameters and table reads.
2. **Residual width and residual tables, a one-knob grid.** [`exp513`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp513_qk_argmax_nap4_tph256) (width 384, 128 tables per layer) with forks [`exp530`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp530_residual_tph256), [`exp562`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp562_D512), [`exp563`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp563_D128), [`exp564`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp564_D512_restph256) and [`exp565`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp565_D512_restph512): each config differs from exp513 only in residual width or residual tables; 1.4825 for the base, 1.5570 at width 128, 1.4513 at width 512 with 512 tables.
   Belongs to "Shape of the tables": the page says the residual contribution prefers deeper tables but has no width result; this grid shows both levers help and each doubling of tables gains less.
3. **HBM bytes per token and wall clock of the LUT model against dense.** [Bandwidth summary](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/summary_2026-06-11_bandwidth.md#L3-L14) compares [`exp750`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp750_FastMHL_exp737v2_clip_all_4K) with the dense [`exp738`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp738_vanilla_bf16_bs48_4k) at 4k steps: 1.3863 against 1.3984, 32.5 against 35.8 MB read per token at deployment, 104 ms against 2.85 ms per forward on an H100.
   Belongs next to the hard-deployment recipe: the only measurement of the "virtual bandwidth" argument (a bandwidth win with no wall-clock win on a GPU), not another loss row.

## [`architectures/transformers/unembedders.md`](../architectures/transformers/unembedders.md)

4. **The big MLP head, isolated.** [`exp174`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp174_lut_qk_big_unembed/config.json#L3) is exp154 with only the head changed from a linear layer to a 3072-wide GELU MLP (+89M parameters): 1.7105 ([`exp154`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/nanochat_exps/exp154_bit_attention)) to 1.6478 (old evaluation, 8k steps, batch 16).
   Belongs to "What was found": it is the size of the head lever in the early BPE models (the page has only the later wide-head test that showed no gain).

## [`architectures/transformers/ffn-replacement.md`](../architectures/transformers/ffn-replacement.md)

5. **Dense layers inside a LUT stack, fixed evaluation, 48k steps.** The pure LUT stack with the total-variation penalty [`exp_n_0238`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/runs_corrected/exp_n_0238_pureLUT0193_TVlam10_48k_seed1) reaches 1.1299; last layer dense [`exp_n_0241`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/runs_corrected/exp_n_0241_pureLUT0193_TVlam10_L5dense_48k_seed1) 1.1234; last two dense [`exp_n_0242`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/runs_corrected/exp_n_0242_pureLUT0193_TVlam10_L4L5dense_48k_seed1) 1.1181; dense reference [`exp_n_0216`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/runs_corrected/exp_n_0216_vanilla48k_gelu_ckpt2k_seed1) 1.1154.
   Belongs to section 4 (parity that does not last): the only runs that mix dense and LUT layers, and they shrink the 48k gap step by step (0.0145 down to 0.0027).
6. **A LUT in place of the attention output projection.** [`exp_o_outproj_lut`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/lut_ablation/exp_o_outproj_lut_B16k_light_lmfrozeng_n2_tau0p5_tph128_seed1/config.json#L3) (its config names the baseline [`exp_g_abl_04`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/c4a750b2f9ab65efe6a61254103a92d6fd8fc2a2/experiments/ffn_replacement/lut_ablation/exp_g_abl_04_B16k_light_lmfrozeng_n2_tau0p5_tph128_seed1)) gives 1.1865 against 1.1600 (fixed evaluation, 16k steps).
   Belongs to section 4: a negative result for the one attention matrix a reader would try to route next.
7. **Dense training pulled toward its LUT.** [`exp_n_0056`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/0c35e3be8deab30338681013158ecbd166548764/experiments/hyperplane_ffn/exp_n_0056_lut_constrained_dense_16k/README.md#L1-L10) to [`exp_n_0070`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/0c35e3be8deab30338681013158ecbd166548764/experiments/hyperplane_ffn/exp_n_0070_lutconstr_batch24576_lam8p0_const): a penalty pulls each dense feed-forward toward a fitted LUT; the dense loss stays at 1.196, but swapping the LUTs in gives 1.3578 (penalty weight 0.1) to 1.3302 (weight 8), against 1.2286 for end-to-end LUT training ([`exp_n_0052`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/0c35e3be8deab30338681013158ecbd166548764/experiments/hyperplane_ffn/exp_n_0052_H8_d48_tph64_batched_control_16k)); old evaluation, 16k steps.
   Belongs beside sections 7 and 8 (distilling into LUTs, a dense network imitating a LUT head): the third direction, and it fails.
8. **Routed value projection.** [VSWEEP summary](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/0c35e3be8deab30338681013158ecbd166548764/experiments/hyperplane_ffn/VSWEEP_SUMMARY.md#L7-L21) and [INNER48 summary](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/0c35e3be8deab30338681013158ecbd166548764/experiments/hyperplane_ffn/INNER48_SWEEP_SUMMARY.md#L3-L10): the compress, LUT, decompress block in place of the dense V projection stays behind a dense V at 3.5k steps (best by 0.0066 at 1.57 times the parameters), but the version without decompress reaches 1.1922 against 1.1987 at 16k steps with about 2.5 times the parameters (old evaluation).
   Belongs as its own short section: the only routed attention projection that ends below its dense counterpart in these summaries, and the summary states the bandwidth-for-matmul trade.

9. **A recurrent LUT cell.** [`exp_lut_rnn`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/blob/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/transformer_exps/exp_lut_rnn/train.py#L46-L55) (state update `0.5 * h + lut(cat(x, h))`, no gates, 4.22M parameters) reaches 1.4721 on bytes at context 32, against 1.4421 for the GRU [`exp_gru_baseline`](https://github.com/anatoli-starostin/spiky/tree/e85a5ceca156e79d199b0f6b395aa8e7e4ee287a/transformer_exps/exp_gru_baseline) (0.92M parameters), same data, steps and batch.
   Belongs to the "Recursive setups" part: the only recurrent LUT result (its LUT GRU sibling has no summary and is not listed).
