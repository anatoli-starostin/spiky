Contents · Discovered by neuroevolution — RS/FS Izhikevich, latency-ordering

A neuroevolution search bred spiking networks against a latency-ordering task: six inputs each fire one spike whose time encodes a value (earlier = larger), and the four output neurons must fire in the order matching an oracle ranking — scored by Kendall tau-b. Neurons are the two canonical Izhikevich-2006 types, RS excitatory and FS inhibitory, assigned per-neuron by evolution (no continuous neuron params). This is the best net found (tau-b 0.796), run live in your browser with the faithful quadratic-Izhikevich update.

About this network

Best genome from the neuroevo_izh2 population (Kendall tau-b 0.796, sole fitness) — a compact 6 inputs → 6 hidden → 4 outputs with 37 synapses (9 RS / 7 FS neurons, delays 1–30 ticks). Full quadratic Izhikevich, v′ = 0.04v²+5v+140−u+I; RS a=0.02,d=8 and FS a=0.1,d=2, threshold 30 mV. Only topology, weights, delays, and each neuron's RS/FS bit were evolved. Constraining neurons to just these two canonical types matched the unconstrained model's score — evidence the search space was over-broad. The in-browser tau-b reproduces the native score to within ~1%.
