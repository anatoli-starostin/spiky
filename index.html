LUT → Spiking networks

A trained lookup-table (LUT) network can be turned into a real spiking network (Izhikevich / SPNet) by direct construction — no retraining — via latency coding: a vector is represented by the first-spike times of one population of neurons, and the constructed spiking circuit re-emits the LUT's output as the spike times of another population.

Walker2d — live spiking controller (interactive stand)

A real Walker2d SAC control policy (32 lookup tables over the 17-D observation) compiled into a latency-coded spiking network and run live in your browser against a real MuJoCo simulation on the server. Pick an actor (LUT-SAC / int4 / SAC baseline), pause & step, toggle free-fall, adjust speed — and watch the decoded actions drive the walker in real time. Each viewer gets its own independent simulation.
